Sealed typefaces

Young Serif (headings, display)   https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Young+Serif
Public Sans (UI, body, wordmark)  https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Public+Sans

Both are free under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org).
The files here are Latin-only cuts made for quick install. For other scripts,
or the variable version of Public Sans, download the full families from Google Fonts.

The wordmark is set in Public Sans at weight 750 with -2.8% letter spacing,
then outlined. Always use the logo files; never retype the wordmark.
